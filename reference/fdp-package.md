# fdp: f-Differential Privacy and Gaussian Differential Privacy

Constructs and visualises trade-off functions for f-differential privacy
(f-DP) as introduced by Dong et al. (2022)
[doi:10.1111/rssb.12454](https://doi.org/10.1111/rssb.12454) . Supports
Gaussian differential privacy, the f-DP generalisation of (epsilon,
delta)-differential privacy, and accepts user-specified optimal type I /
type II errors from which the lower convex hull trade-off function is
automatically constructed.
